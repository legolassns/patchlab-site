2. What information we collect

When you submit the quote request form, we may collect the following information:

  • Full name — required;
  • Company or organisation — optional;
  • Email address — required;
  • Telephone number — optional;
  • Patch type — required;
  • Approximate quantity — required;
  • Application or backing type — optional;
  • Intended date of use — optional;
  • Project description and any additional information you choose to provide — optional.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in the free-text field unless it is strictly necessary for us to assess your request.

3. Why we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • understand and evaluate your project;
  • prepare and send you a quotation;
  • contact you about your request;
  • ask for any additional information required to define the project correctly;
  • manage subsequent communications, samples, approvals and possible reorders;
  • take steps at your request before entering into a contract;
  • perform the resulting contract, where an order is confirmed;
  • comply with legal, accounting and tax obligations;
  • prevent misuse of the website and protect our services and legal rights.

We do not use the information submitted through the quote form for behavioural advertising, unrelated marketing activities or automated profiling.

We do not make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing.

4. Legal basis for processing

Depending on the circumstances, we process your personal data on one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Pre-contractual measures — when you ask us to evaluate a project, provide information or prepare a quotation;
  • Performance of a contract — when you confirm an order or request services from us;
  • Compliance with legal obligations — including accounting, tax and administrative requirements;
  • Legitimate interests — including managing business communications, providing and improving our services, maintaining website security, preventing misuse and protecting our rights.

Providing the information marked as required is necessary for us to understand and respond to your request. Without it, we may be unable to prepare a quotation or contact you.

5. How we handle your information

Personal data is processed using organisational and technical measures designed to protect it against unauthorised access, accidental loss, alteration, disclosure or misuse.

Access is limited to authorised persons who need the information to assess your request, prepare a quotation, manage an order or provide related assistance.

Information submitted through the website is validated by the form system and delivered to the PatchLab email environment used to manage enquiries and related communications.

6. Service providers and recipients

Where necessary, personal data may be processed by carefully selected providers that support the operation of PatchLab and Euroricami, including:

  • hosting and technical infrastructure providers, for website availability, maintenance and security;
  • Zoho Mail, for receiving, sending and managing quote requests and related communications;
  • technical providers strictly necessary for the operation, protection and maintenance of the website;
  • professional advisers, where their assistance is required for accounting, legal, administrative or technical purposes;
  • public authorities or regulatory bodies, where disclosure is required by law.

These recipients receive only the information necessary for the relevant purpose. Where required, service providers process personal data on our behalf under appropriate contractual and data-protection obligations.

We do not sell or rent your personal data.

7. Cookies and analytics

PatchLab uses Plausible Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the website.

Plausible is configured as a privacy-oriented analytics service. It does not use advertising or profiling cookies and does not create visitor profiles for behavioural advertising or cross-site tracking.

The analytics service may record aggregated information such as page views, referral sources, device categories and selected interactions with the website.

Nothing you enter in the quote request form — including your name, company, email address, telephone number, patch specifications, quantity or project notes — is sent to Plausible.

For form measurement, the system may record only technical events such as whether a submission succeeded or failed and which language version of the page was used. The contents of the request are never included in those analytics events.

PatchLab does not use advertising cookies, behavioural targeting or third-party advertising trackers. If the website introduces services that require different technologies in the future, this Privacy Policy and any applicable cookie information will be updated before those technologies are activated.

8. International data transfers

Some service providers may process personal data in countries outside the European Economic Area.

Where an international transfer takes place, it is managed using the safeguards required by applicable data-protection law, such as an adequacy decision, approved contractual clauses or another legally recognised transfer mechanism.

9. How long we keep your information

Quote requests and related communications are normally retained for up to 24 months from the last communication.

This period allows us to manage the project, respond to follow-up questions, retain the context of the quotation and recognise a possible reorder without asking you to provide the same information again.

Information associated with confirmed orders, invoices, accounting records, legal obligations, complaints or disputes may be retained for longer where required by law or where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

At the end of the applicable retention period, personal data is deleted or anonymised unless continued retention is legally required.

10. Your data-protection rights

Subject to the conditions established by applicable data-protection law, you may have the right to:

  • request confirmation as to whether we process personal data relating to you;
  • request access to your personal data and information about how it is processed;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • request deletion of your personal data;
  • request restriction of processing;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • receive personal data you have provided in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, where the right to data portability applies;
  • lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority.

These rights may be subject to legal limitations, including cases where processing or retention is required to comply with an obligation or protect a legal right.

In Italy, the competent supervisory authority is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.
