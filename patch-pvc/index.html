Custom PVC Patches

PVC patches give your design a moulded, raised surface and a technical look. Available in 2D or 3D with solid colours, they can be supplied with hook-and-loop backing or ready to sew onto a garment.

Request a PVC patch quote

A logo and an idea of how you will use the patch are enough to start. We can help you choose the relief and backing.

When to choose a PVC patch

PVC is worth considering when you want defined shapes, solid colours and a raised, technical finish. It is also selected for professional equipment, backpacks and outdoor applications. We assess suitability according to the intended use, exposure and attachment method.

2D or 3D: choose the visual effect

As a visual guide, 2D designs use more distinct, flatter levels, while 3D designs have more sculpted relief. Both are available: the choice depends on the artwork and the effect you want. Construction and thickness are assessed for each project with the specialist producer.

Choose how the patch attaches

The backing depends on the garment and whether the patch needs to be removable. For PVC, these are the configurations we offer.

Hook only

The hook component is attached to the patch. This option needs a matching loop surface already on the garment or equipment.

Hook + loop set

The patch has hook backing and is supplied with the matching loop component. Choose this configuration when you also need the receiving panel.

Sew-on

The patch is supplied ready to be sewn onto the garment. On request, PatchLab/Euroricami can also carry out the sewing directly on the garment.

See how hook-and-loop fastening works.

Colours and artwork

PVC is particularly suited to solid-colour graphics. Pantone references can be used as colour references to guide production. We review the artwork at the intended size before confirming feasibility, including the lettering, fine details and colour layout.

When another technique is a better fit

PVC is not our first choice for very fine details, gradients or designs with many colours. We choose the technique according to the project: simplifying the artwork is one option, and changing technique is another.

For fine details and small lettering, consider Woven HD patches.

For gradients, images or complex colour work, consider sublimated patches.

Want to compare this technique with the others before deciding? Read the guide How to choose the right patch. For the technical terms used on this page, see the glossary or the full index of guides.

From your file to approval

PatchLab manages the project and supply, with PVC manufacturing entrusted to specialist production partners.

  1. 1

    Project review

    Send your logo, intended use, approximate dimensions and quantity. We check the design and agree the relief, colours and backing.

  2. 2

    Digital artwork approval

    We prepare digital artwork or a render for you to review. Production starts after you approve the project.

  3. 3

    Production and supply

    The agreed design goes into production, followed by checking and supply. PatchLab remains your contact for the project.

Quantities and repeat orders

PVC projects typically start from around 100 pieces. This is an indicative commercial starting point: smaller quantities may be assessed case by case.

PatchLab keeps the project information to make repeat orders easier. If the design and configuration stay the same, a reorder can be based on the previous project. Tell us about any changes so we can review what needs updating.

Is PVC the right fit for your project?

Send us your logo, intended use and approximate quantity. We will assess whether PVC is suitable and help you choose the configuration. You do not need to have Pantone references, a backing choice or a decision between 2D and 3D before contacting us.
